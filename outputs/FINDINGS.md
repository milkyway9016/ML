| XSICNEFE_NorthEurope_FarEast | 2829 | 14.5 | +0.007 | +0.194 | 1.001 | +89.3 |
| XSICUWFE_USWestCoast_FarEast | 2071 | 23.7 | +0.002 | +0.096 | 1.000 | +34.3 |

Two facts drive the interpretation:

1. **Lag-1 volatility clustering is absent** (acf(r^2,1) ~ 0.00, VR(5) ~ 1.00). A real market series has acf(r^2,1) of +0.2 to +0.4 and VR(5) of 1.3-2.5. This series does not behave like an observed price process at daily frequency.
2. **Monthly-scale structure IS present** (acf(r^2,21) ~ +0.19). So the target is not pure noise and there is something to learn - it is just not day-to-day volatility persistence, which is what a HAR/GARCH model is built to exploit.

The practical consequence: models that lean on trailing-vol persistence (HAR, EWMA, GARCH) are working with the only autocorrelation that exists, and models that exploit regime-level information are competing on the axis where the signal is.

## 2. Does the ML beat the naive baseline?

The shrunk variants apply `w * ML + (1-w) * HAR` with `w` chosen on a time-ordered validation slice. `w = 0` reproduces HAR exactly, so a challenger that adds nothing is shrunk back to the benchmark rather than reported as a loss.

## 6. Bugs fixed in this version

| # | v1 defect | effect | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 route labels, 2 physical lanes with bit-identical prices | 42% duplicated rows; every t-stat inflated ~sqrt(2) | collapse to 2 canonical lanes |
| 2 | `fillna(0.0)` on every feature | `slp_*` is in Pascals, so its NaNs became a -527 sigma outlier; 42 of 73 weather columns collapsed to constant zero on Pacific lanes | per-lane TRAIN-FOLD median imputation + missingness indicators |
| 3 | one GARCH fitted to all lanes concatenated; horizon = n_test_rows (~84) for a ~21 day month; step k assigned to row k | 63 of 84 forecast steps were fiction; the 'best QLIKE' was a flattest-forecast artifact | per-lane fit; sum the 5 one-step conditional variances |
| 4 | large-move threshold = training-fold q90 | 40 of 75 folds had zero test positives and were silently dropped; only turbulent months were scored | one fixed threshold from a pre-2020 slice |
| 5 | mean QLIKE as headline metric | one row = 90% of the pooled total | median and 1%-trimmed reported; pooled R2 added |
| 6 | 400 boosting rounds, lr 0.03, no validation set | trees extrapolated into the deep tail (log-pred sd 1.13 vs true 1.089) | early stopping on a time-ordered validation slice, small hyperparameter grid, convex shrinkage toward HAR |
| 7 | Newey-West applied to an unsorted pivot | wrong overlap structure | sort by date; add winsorised and date-collapsed variants |
| 8 | `FINDINGS.md` documented but never written | missing deliverable | written |
